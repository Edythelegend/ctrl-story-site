Rhythm Fighter - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Rhythm Fighter app ("the app") handles and why. The app is published by CTRLSTORY S.R.L., a company registered in Romania ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name or email address. The name you give your fighter stays in the game.
  • Your progress is stored on your device and, through Google Play Games, in your Google account so it can follow you to a new phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob and its partners AppLovin and Unity Ads, and sells optional gem packs through Google Play.
  • Crash reports help us fix bugs. Usage statistics are collected only if you consent.
  • If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads and usage statistics. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the app.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, items, statistics and settings (language, sound, calibration and so on) on your device. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Cloud save and achievements (Google Play Games)

The app uses Google Play Games Services, a service of Google, to save a copy of your progress to your Google account and to record achievements. Google signs you in to Play Games automatically on Android. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy. We can't see your Google account details.

Advertising (Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads)

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) to show banner, full-screen and rewarded ads. AdMob may fill an ad slot through its partners AppLovin (AppLovin Corporation) and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To serve, limit, measure and (where permitted) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud, these providers may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as your Android Advertising ID;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, tapped or closed) and diagnostic data.

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. Each provider processes it under its own policy: how Google uses information from apps that use its services, AppLovin, Unity.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) sends us a crash report: what the app was doing, the device model, the operating system version and a random installation identifier. We use it only to find and fix bugs, based on our legitimate interest in a working app (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Crash reports are kept for 90 days. You can turn them off under Settings → Privacy options.

Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

Only if you consent, Firebase Analytics (Google) collects how the app is used: sessions, screens, the app version, device information, the approximate region, a random app-instance identifier, and a few game milestones (for example stage progress and best scores). We use it to understand which parts of the game work and which need improving. We keep this data for 2 months. See Privacy and security in Firebase.

Purchases (Google Play, RevenueCat)

Gem packs are paid through Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check and restore purchases, the app sends the purchase receipt and a random customer identifier to RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which processes it under its privacy policy. The country of your Google Play account is used to hide paid random rewards where local rules require it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app reminds you on the phone itself, for example when your energy is full or a building is finished; these reminders don't send any data. News about game events is sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), which gives your installation a random token so the message can reach it. You can turn notifications off at any time in your Android settings.

Other internet requests

To keep daily rewards fair, the app asks timeapi.io for the current time, or our own website if that fails. It also downloads the list of game events and a few game settings (Firebase Remote Config) so we can adjust them without an update. Like any internet request, these reveal your IP address to the server that answers; no other data is sent.

Consent and legal basis

For users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app uses Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) on first launch. Personalized ads and usage statistics are used only if you consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). If you do not consent, non-personalized ads may still be shown. These rely on limited data used to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, based on our legitimate interest in funding a free game (Art. 6(1)(f)). Cloud save, purchases and crash reports rely on providing the game you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in keeping it working (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can change your choices at any time under Settings → Privacy options.

You can also reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads for all apps, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside the EU, mainly in the United States. They do so under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, please contact us.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent. Because we hold almost no data that identifies you, many requests are best directed to the providers above. You can still contact us and we will help. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP).

Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the app adds features. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
